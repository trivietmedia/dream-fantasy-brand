## Publishing
Schedule Friday 20:00 Vietnam time (about 09:00 US Eastern / 06:00 US Pacific; one hour earlier in the US after Nov 1). Upload the day before and use Schedule in YouTube Studio. Check Analytics → Audience after 5–10 videos and adjust.
Policy notes: keep each video visually and musically distinct (repetitive, mass-produced content can be refused monetization); create music on a paid Suno plan if it will be monetized; verify the channel by phone to upload custom thumbnails.
